Section I — Scope of service
PAGE 2

A brokerage that reads like a desk, not a call center.

Burke works by email, because that's how real estate transactions already move. Send what you have. We'll tell you what's missing and pull the rest.

1.1

Intake by email

Property address, loan amount or dwelling minimum, closing date, policy type, borrower and client details, mortgagee clause if financed. Missing something? We ask in writing so you have a record of it.

1.2

Property data pulled, not requested

Square footage, year built, construction, roof — we source what's available rather than sending an investor back to their files for details we can find.

1.3

Classified to the right form

Landlord, builders risk, vacant, short-term rental, commercial, general liability, homeowners. Vacant and builders risk are not interchangeable, and the wrong one shows up at claim time.

1.4

Quoted across every fitting appetite

No house carrier. Each market whose appetite matches the risk gets the submission, and the comparison goes back to the client with price on the table.

1.5

Bound, issued, and revised on the clock

Once the EOI is out, changes still come. Those revisions are treated as deadline work, not as an afterthought.

Section II — Coverage lines
PAGE 3

Written for property that changes state.

An investment property is rarely one thing for long. It closes vacant, sits through a renovation, gets a tenant, then goes on a nightly rental platform for a season. Each of those is a different underwriting conversation, and a policy that fit in March may not respond in September.

VersiFi places landlord policies for held rentals, builders risk for ground-up and renovation work, vacant forms for the gap between, short-term rental coverage for nightly use, and commercial policies where the portfolio has outgrown the residential forms.

General liability is placed on its own where a client needs the liability side separated from the property. Homeowners sits alongside for owner-occupied files, including Texas windstorm placement where the coast requires a separate association policy.

Life insurance rounds out the book for investors carrying leverage. A balance sheet built on financed property has a person behind it, and that exposure is worth naming.
